What's Included

TOP TO BOTTOM.

EVERYTHING IN STANDARD

All kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, vacuuming, mopping, dusting, and trash removal. The full standard clean, included.

INSIDE OVEN

Oven interior degreased and scrubbed clean. Racks removed and wiped. Glass door cleaned inside and out.

INSIDE REFRIGERATOR

All shelves, drawers, and door compartments removed, washed, and replaced. Interior walls wiped down.

BASEBOARDS & TRIM

Every baseboard and door trim scrubbed with a damp cloth, not just dusted. Gets the grime that collects over time.

VENTS & AIR RETURNS

All ceiling vents and air return covers wiped or vacuumed. Removes built-up dust that circulates through your air.

WINDOW SILLS & TRACKS

Window sills, ledges, and tracks cleaned out. Removes dirt, dead bugs, and grime that collects in corners.

CABINET INTERIORS

Kitchen and bathroom cabinet interiors wiped down. Removes crumbs, spills, and dust from shelves and corners.
