HOW MUCH DOES HOUSE CLEANING COST IN INDIANAPOLIS?

A straight answer on Indianapolis cleaning prices, what drives them up or down, and why hourly quotes so often end up higher than they first look.

Get an Instant Quote

The short answer

For a typical Indianapolis-area home, a standard recurring clean generally lands somewhere between $110 and $300 depending on the size of the house. A first-time deep clean usually runs roughly one and a half to two times a standard clean, because it covers built-up areas that routine cleaning never reaches. Move-out cleans price similarly to deep cleans, sometimes slightly lower when the property is empty.

Those are real ranges, not teaser numbers. The spread is wide because a two-bedroom condo in Fishers and a five-bedroom house in Zionsville are genuinely different jobs, and any company quoting both at the same price is either overcharging one or cutting corners on the other.

What actually moves the price

Bedrooms and bathrooms matter more than square footage. Bathrooms are the most labour-intensive rooms in any house — a 2,000 sq ft home with four bathrooms takes materially longer than a 2,600 sq ft home with two.

Condition matters as much as size. A home cleaned every two weeks stays close to its baseline, so each visit is predictable. A home that has gone six months between cleans needs a reset first, and pricing that visit as a standard clean is how companies end up rushing or asking for more money on the day.

Frequency changes the per-visit price. Weekly and biweekly schedules cost less per visit than one-off cleans, because the home never drifts far from baseline. One-time cleans carry the full reset cost every time.

Flat-rate versus hourly

Hourly pricing looks cheaper on the phone and frequently isn't. If a company quotes $45 an hour and sends two cleaners for four hours, that is $360 — but you don't find out until the work is done. The incentive also runs the wrong way: slower work earns more.

Flat-rate pricing quotes the home, not the clock. You know the number before anyone arrives, and if the job takes longer than expected that is the company's problem rather than yours. Novo's Cleaning prices flat-rate by home size for exactly this reason.

The one honest advantage of hourly is very small or very specific jobs — a single room, or a couple of hours of focused help. For a whole-home clean, flat-rate is almost always the better deal for the customer.

What should be included at any price

Supplies and equipment should be included. If you are being asked to provide a vacuum or cleaning products, that should be reflected in a lower price.

Insurance is not optional. Anyone working unsupervised in your home should be insured and background-checked, and should be willing to say so plainly when asked.

Travel within the normal service area should not be a separate line item. Surprise trip charges are one of the most common complaints about cleaning quotes in the metro.
