What's Included

EVERY INCH COVERED.

Built for move-outs. We go room by room and clean every surface, corner, and fixture so the property is truly ready for the next person walking through.

EVERYTHING IN DEEP CLEAN

Full standard + deep clean included: all surfaces, floors, bathrooms, inside oven & fridge, baseboards, vents, window sills.

WALLS SPOT-CLEANED

Scuffs, fingerprints, and marks cleaned off walls throughout. Focus on high-touch areas and anywhere visible to inspectors.

ALL CABINET INTERIORS

Every kitchen and bathroom cabinet and drawer scrubbed inside, including corners, shelves, and door edges. Left spotless and odor-free.

INSIDE ALL APPLIANCES

Oven interior, refrigerator interior, and dishwasher interior cleaned. Microwave inside and out. Range hood filter degreased.

LIGHT FIXTURES & SWITCHES

Light fixture covers wiped (especially kitchen and bath). Switch plates and outlet covers cleaned. Often-missed by standard cleans.

CLOSETS SWEPT & WIPED

All closet floors swept and shelves wiped. Rods dusted. Closet corners vacuumed. Leaves no trace of the previous occupants.

GARAGE (IF APPLICABLE)

Garage floor swept and blown out. Walls wiped if accessible. Leaves the garage broom-clean as landlords typically require.
